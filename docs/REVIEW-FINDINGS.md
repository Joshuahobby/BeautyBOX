**Method:** a background multi-agent review (workflow `beautybox-core-review`) ran four read-only reviewers, one per risk dimension. Every reported finding was then challenged by an independent verifier told to refute it and to default to "not real".

- One verifier (product image URLs) did not finish because of a session limit. I assessed that finding myself and fixed it.
