| DR-043 | 10-24 | pawaPay sandbox run (founder's sandbox token; no callbacks, no request signing yet), and refund handling changed by what it showed and by the review that followed. (1) Refund refusals are read in three groups. "Not now" (nothing created; the same id is retried, the job never gives up, `REFUNDS_STUCK` after 24 h): another refund in progress (the sandbox answers `INVALID_STATE` "A refund is already in progress for this deposit.", the docs `REFUND_IN_PROGRESS`), `PROVIDER_TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE`, and our own access problems (`AUTHENTICATION_ERROR`, `AUTHORISATION_ERROR`, `NO_AUTHENTICATION`, `HTTP_SIGNATURE_ERROR`, `REFUNDS_NOT_ALLOWED`; a 401/403 counts as a refusal with or without `status`). "Records disagree" (`INVALID_AMOUNT`, `INVALID_STATE` other than in progress, e.g. "already refunded", `DEPOSIT_NOT_COMPLETED`, `DEPOSIT_NOT_FOUND`, `NOT_FOUND`): failed and flagged `NEEDS_REVIEW`. Anything else: failed, not flagged. (2) "In progress" while the app has nothing else in flight for the deposit means a refund made outside the app: retried and flagged. (3) A refund flagged `NEEDS_REVIEW` keeps its amount reserved, even when failed, and its case stays open, until finance resolves it (CC-18: `AS_RECORDED` clears the flag, for a failed refund only when no refund of the payment was made by any other route; `PAID_BY_PROVIDER` records a failed refund as paid for the amount actually paid back, at most its own, with the provider's reference kept on the refund and the approved amount in the audit). Closing a review re-checks every open case of the payment, and money still owed for a cancelled order, an overpayment or a review receipt gets a case again; a staff-request case reused by a cancellation takes on the cancellation, so it cannot close while money is owed. The "in progress" flag is raised once per refund, so a review finance closed is not reopened by the next retry. The reservation is in the app's budget check under the receipt lock, not the DB trigger, so a provider message can always be recorded. (4) A failed outcome (status check or refusal) for a refund that a verified callback called "completed" is flagged whichever arrived first: the check runs under the receipt lock, which the callback path also takes before deciding, and a "completed" status for a refund already recorded as failed flags it too. A late initiation refusal never overrides a refund another run already moved on. Every refund writer locks receipt then refund. (5) Flagged refunds are shown on the order page (with provider ids, CC-19, and warnings on the refund dialogs), the finance page (paged), as order exception `REFUND_NEEDS_REVIEW`, as ops alert `REFUNDS_NEED_REVIEW` until resolved, and to the customer as "under review" when failed. | Sandbox: rejected ids are not stored and the same id is accepted again; the in-progress, over-refund, already-refunded and failed-deposit answers; a bad token answers 401 with only `failureReason`; statuses ACCEPTED, PROCESSING, IN_RECONCILIATION, COMPLETED, FAILED; calls under a second.
